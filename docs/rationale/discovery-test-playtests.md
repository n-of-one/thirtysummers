# Discovery test playtests

The play log of the discovery test, 5 and 6 Sep 2026, kept as it was
written. It is the evidence for the verdict and for what the first five
summers change. [design.md](design.md) says what was concluded from it. The
log for the step being built now is [../current/PLAYTEST.md](../current/PLAYTEST.md).

The first three sessions answered four questions: when I broke through, did
I feel anything? Which barrier felt best, the mud, the thicket or the
stream? Did I see the field before I reached it? Did the first bridge feel
earned? The last session, over three summers, used a longer set of seven.

**Date** 2026-09-05
**Map** a
**Who played** Erik
**Watched by** none

**What happened**
- finding the twine in the mud was nice. Having a resource being rare and in a soft-locked region already felt like i was getting something for my struggle to go through the mud.
- cutting through the thicket felt satisfying, even when it was not unlocking a new resource, but more so to unlock sticks.
- building a bridge was frustrating as it did not build where I wanted to, and I ended up not having enough sticks to unlock the left side of the map. I got anxious for the amount of resources

## map b, 2026-09-06

**Map** b **Summers played** · **Who played** Erik **Watched by** none
**Build** — none

**Background music** https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-anime-background-japanese-aikoto-1-470639/

**What happened**, in order. Where you went first, what you tried, what
stopped you, what you did about it. Note every place you hesitate
turned back.

year 1: explored the starter area (fun). 
explored the rivers to figure out where to best build the bridge
harvested the vine
found a spot where the thicket was only 2 tiles wide (felt nice)
collected enough for 4 bridge pieces
made bridge and collected gold, made second bridge to found out the two areas behind the rivers are actually one bigger 
area. Collecting gold was nice

Then I found the tiny stream sw, build bridge to collect 1 gold. It was a bit tight with time to go back collect twine and sticks
but I got it done it time.

year 2: 
was a bit disappointed that everything was as I left it, only the resources were there. Collected everythign. finished with 2 minutes 
to spare

year 3:
it turned more into optimal path to gather resources game. Collected everything with 2.5 minutes to spare


Overall: was frustrated with how long the stomach full status is, and how little the fruit fills up stamina. Running is
something that I enjoy (it might be a bit overpowered), but in my mind you should not use it in underbrush (though I 
don't this mathematically matters).

there was a bit of juggling backpack space fruit/water vs collecting gold


**The questions.** Answer while it is fresh. Moments, not verdicts.

1. The moment you felt most. What were you doing, and what was the feeling?
frustration with fruit. I had several moments where I pressed F only to be reminded that I still needed to wait 30s for
this to be possible.


2. Rank the three barriers. One line each on why it sits where it

- thicket: satisfying to hack through it, especially finding a spot where it was only 2 wide. It helps that inside the 
thicket is a cluster of trees that has another mini puzzle: find the sticks behind the trees. 
- mud is ok. could be better if the are you have to plow through is bigger / effort is higher. There was one twine 
really at the edge of the mud. Visually it is pleasing.

   change where you went next?
I scouted the areas beyond the river

4. The first bridge: what did it cost you in trips and in time, and what
   did you get for it? Would you pay it again?

   not expect?
- I expected the thicket to grow back in year 2.
- I did not expect a 2 wide section of thicket (most is 3 wide)


6. Did the clock ever matter? When did you first look at it?
Year 1 I was a bit tight on time, looked at the clock when I found the stream sw and realized I might run out of time

7. Did you press "Next summer"? If yes, what did you do on the open map
   If no, why not.

yes. Made it into a collection efficiency game
