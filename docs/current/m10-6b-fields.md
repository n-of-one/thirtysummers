# M10.6b: the fields in the valley

**Not designed yet.** This file has what M10.6a leaves for it. It gets its
steps once M10.6a has been played and the valley walked.

[M10.6a](m10-6a-valley.md) builds the valley with only the near ring's food
in it. This milestone puts the rest of the table from
[five-summers.md](five-summers.md) back into it, and switches the field
tests back on.

## What goes back

- **The bramble bay and its 4 sticks,** in camp's part. `findBrambleBay`
  takes spots by distance from camp, and `carveBrambleBay` keeps brambles 5
  tiles off the half circle. Both have to measure from the near ring's own
  edge and the water instead.
- **The vines' pocket,** in camp's part. `Wet.pocket` looks for it by angle
  and distance from camp, on the side away from the copse. Both have to be
  replaced by something that follows camp's part.
- **The feather field,** on the open bank, across the stream from camp.
- **The copse and the shells behind it,** further up the open bank, since
  the far bank is closed for now. The strip of grass that is the one way in
  to the shell field comes back with them.
- **The tests** switched off in M10.6a, measuring the same table.

## Open

- How far up the open bank the feather field and the copse sit. In the old
  layout they were 20 and about 45 tiles past the stream. The open bank is
  about 25,000 tiles, and the walk is what summers 2 and 3 are made of.
- Whether the side valley off the open bank's west wall is where the copse
  goes, so that it is a place and not a disc on open ground.
- Whether the far bank gets anything visible from the open bank, as a tease
  for later, the way the shells showed through the copse.
- How many ponds the open bank has, and where, once hydration has been felt
  there in M10.6a.
