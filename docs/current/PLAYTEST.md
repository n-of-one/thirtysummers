# Playtest log: the first five summers

One entry per session: what was attempted, what happened, one sentence on how
it felt. Written at the time, not from memory. What is being tested is in
[five-summers.md](five-summers.md). The discovery test's log, which the
verdict came from, is
[../rationale/discovery-test-playtests.md](../rationale/discovery-test-playtests.md).

How to run one:

```
npm run dev        # then open with ?map=<name>
```

Play it blind. Do not open the map file, and leave the debug overlay shut
until the session is over. The seed box and the teleport are for building,
not for playing.

## Entry template

Copy this for each session.

## map <name>, <date>

**Map** · **Summers played** · **Who played** · **Watched by** ·
**Build**: what changed since the last entry, one line, or "nothing".

**What happened**, summer by summer. What each one opened, what stopped you,
what you did about it. Note every place you hesitated or turned back.

**The questions.** Answer while it is fresh. Moments, not verdicts.

1. Did each summer feel different from the one before? Where did one feel
   like the last one again?
2. Which summer felt best and which worst, and why?
3. Each winter: what did you buy? Did the price of what you could not buy
   yet give the next summer a goal? What did you untick, and why?
4. Did the list steer you, or feel like a list of errands? Did you ever
   ignore it to go and look at something?
5. Did upkeep ever matter? If you missed it, what did that cost?
6. Which wall took more than one summer, and did the second summer feel
   like continuing it or starting again?
7. Did the fog ever push you back to water? Did the well change where you
   worked?
8. The cart: did making a road for it feel like play or like hauling?
9. What did you expect that did not happen? What happened that you did not
   expect?

**Got in the way.** A key that did nothing, a prompt that said the wrong
thing, something you looked for and could not find, a place where you did
not know what to do next.

**One sentence on how it felt.**

**Summary cards**, pasted whole, one per summer.

**Observer notes**, only when someone else played:
- First thing they did after spawning.
- Every place they stood still for more than a few seconds, and what they
  were looking at.
- Anything they said out loud, verbatim.
- What they never found or never used.
- What they asked you, and what you refused to answer.
