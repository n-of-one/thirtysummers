# Playtest log: the first five summers

One entry per session: what was attempted, what happened, one sentence on how
it felt. Written at the time, not from memory. What is being tested is in
[five-summers.md](five-summers.md). The discovery test's log, which the
verdict came from, is
[../rationale/discovery-test-playtests.md](../rationale/discovery-test-playtests.md).

How to run one:

```
npm run dev        # then open localhost:5173, which rolls a seed for you
```

The bare address picks a seed under 1000 and shows it in the address bar.
`?seed=<n>` loads a particular seed, and `?map=<name>` loads a map file if
one was made. Write the seed, or the file, in the entry, so the same map can
be played again. A seed gives the same map only until the generator
changes. To keep a map across a change, save it as a file with
`npm run map -- <n> > public/maps/<name>.txt`.

Play it blind. Do not open a map file, and leave the debug overlay shut
until the session is over. The seed box and the teleport are for building,
not for playing.

## M10, the first three years, 23 Sep 2026

**Map** seeds, not noted · **Summers played** 1 to 3 · **Who played** Erik ·
**Watched by** nobody · **Build**: M10 as its file described it.

Written up from Erik's notes on the session rather than at the keyboard, so it
is shorter than the template asks for and has only what he said.

**What happened.**

*Year 1* is all right. The ring inside the stream is explored, and most of the
work is dodging underbrush so as not to be slowed down. The back and forth to
camp is bearable. Working out how to build the bridge is the fun part. There is
a little too much fruit to collect.

*Year 2* is where it breaks down. The walk from camp to the bridge is too far,
and this is where the exploring is supposed to start. Picking fruit and walking
it back to camp is a chore within a few trips. It is hard to remember where
everything is, so it does not feel like uncovering new terrain. With the
numbers as they were, doing everything in year 2 is not possible: the axe and
the family's next level together need more than the feathers out there, so the
balance has to come from selling sticks and vines, which is not what the fields
are meant to be for.

*Year 3* is more of the same. Too much upkeep, 12 fruit and 10 feathers, before
any progress can be made, and too much walking back and forth.

**One sentence on how it felt.** The first year works and the second and third
are a slog.

**What it decided.** The idea is not in question; the first three years are.
The verdict and the six changes that answer it are in
[../rationale/design.md](../rationale/design.md) and in
[PLAN.md](PLAN.md), as M10.1 to M10.6. Nothing goes on top of the first three
years until they are fun.

## Entry template

Copy this for each session.

## map <name>, <date>

**Map** · **Summers played** · **Who played** · **Watched by** ·
**Build**: what changed since the last entry, one line, or "nothing".

**What happened**, summer by summer. What each one opened, what stopped you,
what you did about it. Note every place you hesitated or turned back.

**The questions.** Answer while it is fresh. Moments, not verdicts.

1. Did each summer feel different from the one before? Where did one feel
   like the last one again?
2. Which summer felt best and which worst, and why?
3. Each winter: what did you buy? Did the price of what you could not buy
   yet give the next summer a goal? What did you untick, and why?
4. Did the list steer you, or feel like a list of errands? Did you ever
   ignore it to go and look at something?
5. Did upkeep ever matter? If you missed it, what did that cost?
6. Which wall took more than one summer, and did the second summer feel
   like continuing it or starting again?
7. Did the fog ever push you back to water? Did the well change where you
   worked?
8. The cart: did making a road for it feel like play or like hauling?
9. What did you expect that did not happen? What happened that you did not
   expect?

**Got in the way.** A key that did nothing, a prompt that said the wrong
thing, something you looked for and could not find, a place where you did
not know what to do next.

**One sentence on how it felt.**

**Summary cards**, pasted whole, one per summer.

**Observer notes**, only when someone else played:
- First thing they did after spawning.
- Every place they stood still for more than a few seconds, and what they
  were looking at.
- Anything they said out loud, verbatim.
- What they never found or never used.
- What they asked you, and what you refused to answer.
